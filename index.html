Reflection: The hardest Grid concept for me was the span keyword. At first I was confused about which direction it would go — whether the item would stretch across columns, down the rows, or somewhere diagonal — and it took some trial and error in practice before I understood that grid-column: span 2 stretches an item sideways across two columns. One thing I had to look up while building was the fr unit, and I learned that it means a "fraction" of the free space, so 1fr columns share the leftover room equally and stay flexible instead of being fixed in pixels. Outside of this class, I have seen this same photo-grid pattern before in my ITD 110 class, where images were arranged in a neat responsive grid. Building this gallery myself helped me finally understand how those layouts hold together and rearrange on different screen sizes.
